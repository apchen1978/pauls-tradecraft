LEAD DISCOVERY · HUMAN HANDOFF

名單出來後，
Paul 怎麼接手？

不是把候選公司照順序寄信。先看產品能說什麼、不能說什麼，再挑少數值得研究的買家，寫下理由和第一個該問的問題。

示範／成效未驗證 · 公司與情境皆為虛構
產品條件

美式純紙牆紙；FOB Shanghai 每卷 USD 10–15（約 6 平方米）；MOQ 1,000 直米（LM）；可訂製。

商業目標

探索美國、歐洲的進口商與大型設計公司；希望首單達一個 20 呎貨櫃。

先確認

每卷價格、直米起訂量與貨櫃目標是不同單位；幅寬、卷長、裝櫃量尚未核對，不能直接換算。

先判斷賣點，不先承諾

可測試的切入點

「較小批量也能訂製」可能比單純低價更有吸引力。示範假設把歐美供應商放在 FOB 每卷約 USD 12–25、起訂量較高的位置；這不是已核實的市場行情。

Paul 會先核對

同規格、品質、印刷與認證要求是否可比？USD 10–15 與 USD 12–25 有重疊，不能說我們一定更便宜；也不能把「可訂製」直接等同買家會採購。

先看這三家，理由各不相同

以下公司、需求和判斷均為虛構示範，不是查得的真實買家訊號，也沒有進行接觸。

候選 A · 品牌／進口

Kensington Pattern Co.

倫敦 · 虛構品牌商兼進口商

為什麼先看

設定為正在找紙基牆紙代工，重視印刷品質，訂量有機會達整櫃；產品類型與目標接近。

第一次接觸角度

先談紙基印刷與小批量訂製的匹配，確認對方是否真在評估新代工來源；不先承諾品質或整櫃量。

還要問清楚

  • 預計年用量與單次採購量？
  • 印刷品質、打樣與認證要求？
  • 需要多少個訂製花色？
候選 B · 進口批發

Harbor & Vine Wallcoverings

紐澤西 · 虛構進口批發商

為什麼先看

設定為主售美式花色，正在考慮第二貨源降低成本；可能有補足現有供應的需求。

第一次接觸角度

從第二貨源能否補其花色和採購彈性談起，而不是只報較低價格；先問清楚目前缺口。

還要問清楚

  • 現有花色與目標價格帶？
  • 年用量、採購批量與付款條件？
  • 第二貨源需要哪些測試或認證？
候選 C · 專案設計

Redwood Line Design

加州 · 虛構高端住宅設計公司

為什麼先看

設定為有明確訂製需求，單案量小、全年有多個專案；可測試客製方向，但未必符合首單整櫃目標。

第一次接觸角度

先了解專案的花色、打樣節奏和訂量；把它視為客製合作假設，不把多案加總當作已成立的整櫃訂單。

還要問清楚

  • 單案與全年預計用量？
  • 花色開發數量與打樣時程？
  • 住宅專案有無特定材料或認證要求？

這三家先不投入，不是名單越長越好

Lumen Studio Group
Bayview Hospitality Design虛構的酒店／商用設計需求偏向商用乙烯壁布與防火認證；這份純紙產品條件目前不相符。除非規格被重新確認，不把它們排在首輪。
Atlas Home Supply虛構要求是每卷低於 USD 8、付款 90 天；與示範售價和現有付款風險邊界不合，先不投入。

Paul 接手的交付

三家候選各有「為什麼是它」、第一個接觸角度與待問問題；三家暫緩也寫下原因。這是接觸前的人工判斷示範，不是開發信、已寄出的訊息或成交證據。

After the list, what does Paul do?

He does not email candidates in list order. First, he checks what the product can and cannot credibly offer; then he chooses a few accounts worth studying and records why, what angle to explore, and what to ask.

Demonstration / outcomes unverified · All companies and scenarios are fictional
Product

American-style pure-paper wallpaper; FOB Shanghai USD 10–15 per roll (about 6 m²); MOQ 1,000 linear meters (LM); customization available.

Commercial goal

Explore importers and large design firms in the US and Europe; aim for a first order of one 20-foot container.

Check first

Roll price, linear-meter MOQ, and container volume are different units. Width, roll length, and loading capacity have not been checked; no direct conversion is assumed.

Test the angle before making a promise

A possible opening

“Customization at a smaller volume” may matter more than a low price alone. This fictional comparison assumes US and European suppliers at roughly USD 12–25 FOB per roll with higher MOQs. It is not verified market pricing.

Paul would verify

Are specifications, print quality, and certification requirements comparable? The USD 10–15 and USD 12–25 ranges overlap, so a lower price is not guaranteed. Customization does not itself prove buyer demand.

Three accounts, three different reasons

These names, needs, and judgments are fictional examples—not researched buyer signals. No outreach has taken place.

Candidate A · Brand / importer

Kensington Pattern Co.

London · fictional brand and importer

Why it makes the short list

Assumed to be seeking a paper-wallpaper manufacturing partner, to care about print quality, and to have potential container-scale volume. The product and goal may align.

First-contact angle

Explore the fit between paper printing and smaller-batch customization; first confirm whether a new manufacturing source is under consideration. Do not promise quality or volume yet.

Still to ask

  • Annual usage and order size?
  • Print, sample, and certification requirements?
  • How many custom patterns?
Candidate B · Import wholesaler

Harbor & Vine Wallcoverings

New Jersey · fictional importer and wholesaler

Why it makes the short list

Assumed to focus on American-style patterns and to be considering a second source to lower costs. There may be a gap in its current supply.

First-contact angle

Explore whether a second source can fill a pattern or ordering-flexibility gap, rather than leading only with a lower price. Verify the gap first.

Still to ask

  • Current range and target price band?
  • Annual volume, order size, and payment terms?
  • Testing or certification for a second source?
Candidate C · Project design

Redwood Line Design

California · fictional high-end residential design firm

Why it makes the short list

Assumed to have specific customization needs, small individual projects, and several projects a year. It can test the custom-work angle, but may not meet the first-container goal.

First-contact angle

Clarify patterns, sample timing, and project volume first. Treat this as a custom-work hypothesis, not a container order assembled from imagined future projects.

Still to ask

  • Volume per project and per year?
  • Pattern count and sampling lead time?
  • Any material or certification requirements?

Three to set aside for now

Lumen Studio Group
Bayview Hospitality DesignFictional hospitality and commercial projects favor contract vinyl wallcovering with fire certification. That does not match the pure-paper offer as described. Do not prioritize without clarifying the specification.
Atlas Home SupplyThe fictional requirement is below USD 8 per roll with 90-day payment. It does not match the sample price range or the assumed payment-risk boundary.

What Paul hands over

Each selected account has a reason, a first-contact angle, and open questions. Each deferred account has a reason too. This is an example of human judgment before contact—not an outreach email, sent message, or evidence of a sale.
