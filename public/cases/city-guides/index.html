03 · Evidence discipline

事實、推論與未知分開

FACT

來源原頁可見，附網址和日期。

INFERENCE

從事實推出的判斷，明確標為推論。

UNKNOWN

查不到或頁面打不開，就保留未知，不寫成沒有。

04 · Review

審查如何抓出問題

以下是整理檔案時留下的四個審查例子，並非市場成效。

01 壁紙還是裝飾膜

一個候選被標成壁紙工廠；審查發現官網所查頁面主要是裝飾膜，壁紙資格仍需確認。

02 辦公室不是工廠

另一候選的廣州地址是辦公室；工廠位置沒有被原頁確認，不能直接歸入珠三角工廠。

03 打不開的來源

審查記錄憑證主機名稱不符與網域解析失敗；讀不到的頁面不能當作已核實的來源。

04 保留零結果

一次補查沒有合格資料列，另有 18 筆放棄名單。名單數來自檔案計數，不是工時；未以平台頁湊數。

05 · Process

從問題到指南的十步

  1. 先定義證據、候選與不可寫的內容。
  2. 把問題拆成欄位固定的小任務。
  3. AI 查公開資料，結果存成新檔。
  4. 人或另一個 AI 對照原頁抽查。
  5. 缺口標為 UNKNOWN，不補猜。
  6. 由人決定補查、換方法或暫緩。
  7. 整理成 HTML，再排版輸出 PDF。
  8. 換城市時沿用問題，重新查證據。
  9. 公開前將業者名稱去識別化。
  10. 保留版本與決定，方便追溯。

06 · Limits

限制與尚未量測的部分

  • 這是方法研究，不是客戶交付；沒有客戶採用數據。
  • 未量測不用 AI 的基準時間，不主張節省時間或成本。
  • 部分稅則與原產地證書的官方原文仍待取得，指南中保留未知。
  • 公開 PDF 的業者名稱已去識別化。

02 · Roles

What the person and AIs each do

A person sets the question, makes tradeoffs and signs off. AI findings still need checking against their sources.

Paul / decision-maker

Sets the question, decides tradeoffs and further checks, and signs off.

Does not treat AI conclusions as facts.

Codex

Uses task cards to research public factory, supplier and freight pages.

Does not contact people, estimate or guess domains.

GrokBot

Finds official texts on origin certificates, sales tax and rates.

Does not make the commercial decision.

Claude Code

Writes task cards, spot-checks sources, records review notes and lays out the guides.

Does not change governance originals or publish without approval.

03 · Evidence discipline

Separate facts, inferences and unknowns

FACT

Visible on the source page, with a URL and date.

INFERENCE

A judgment derived from facts, explicitly labelled as an inference.

UNKNOWN

When information is not found or a page cannot be opened, keep it unknown rather than asserting absence.

04 · Review

How review exposed gaps

These four examples come from the review record; they are not market outcomes.

01 Wallpaper or decorative film

One candidate was labelled a wallpaper factory; review found the pages checked focused on decorative films. Its wallpaper qualification still needs confirmation.

02 An office is not a factory

Another candidate's Guangzhou address was an office. The source did not establish the factory location, so it could not be counted as a Pearl River Delta factory.

03 Sources that could not be opened

The review recorded a certificate hostname mismatch and a domain resolution failure. An unreadable page cannot serve as a verified source.

04 Keeping a zero result

One follow-up produced no qualifying rows and a list of 18 rejected candidates. The count comes from a file count, not working hours; platform pages were not used to fill the gap.

05 · Process

Ten steps from question to guide

  1. Define evidence, candidate criteria and what must not be claimed.
  2. Break the question into small tasks with fixed fields.
  3. AI researches public sources and saves new files.
  4. A person or another AI spot-checks original pages.
  5. Mark gaps UNKNOWN rather than guessing.
  6. A person decides whether to check further, change methods or hold.
  7. Prepare HTML, then lay out the PDF.
  8. Reuse the questions for another city and research its evidence anew.
  9. Anonymise company names before publication.
  10. Keep versions and decisions traceable.

06 · Limits

Limits and what was not measured

  • This is method research, not a client delivery; there is no customer adoption data.
  • No baseline without AI was measured, so no time or cost saving is claimed.
  • Some official tariff and certificate-of-origin texts remain outstanding and are marked unknown in the guides.
  • Company names in the public PDFs have been anonymised.
