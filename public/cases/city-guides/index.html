Method demonstration · Human + AI

把模糊的商業問題，
變成可以拍板的研究

用一個真實的貿易題目當練習場，示範我如何和多個 AI 分工、如何分級證據、如何保留「不知道」。題目本身與結論不公開；公開的是方法。

方法示範／成效未驗證 · 非客戶交付

02 · Roles

人和 AI 各自負責什麼

由人設定問題、取捨與簽核，AI 的資料與結論仍須對照來源。

Paul／負責人

定題目、決定取捨與是否補查，最後簽核。

不把 AI 結論直接當成事實。

Codex

依任務卡查公開網頁，整理資料。

不聯絡他人、不估算、不補猜網域。

GrokBot

查找官方原文，例如法規與規則。

不替負責人下商業結論。

Claude Code

寫任務卡、抽查來源、整理審查筆記並排版。

不擅自改治理正本或發佈。

03 · Evidence discipline

事實、推論與未知分開

FACT

來源原頁可見，附網址和日期。

INFERENCE

從事實推出的判斷，明確標為推論。

UNKNOWN

查不到或頁面打不開，就保留未知，不寫成沒有。

04 · Review

審查如何抓出問題

以下是整理檔案時留下的四個審查例子，並非市場成效。

01 分類與實際不符

一個候選被標成符合條件；審查打開官網原頁，發現主要產品是另一種，資格仍需確認。

02 地址不等於產地

另一候選的公開地址是辦公室，頁面沒有自稱生產；審查改標為「候選，生產地未知」。

03 費率表的用途不同

一份公開費率表其實是專案專用，不是一般報價；審查把它降為量級參考，不當作報價使用。

04 保留零結果

一次補查沒有合格資料列，另有 18 筆放棄名單與原因。名單數來自檔案計數，不是工時；未以替代來源湊數。

05 · Process

從問題到成品的十步

  1. 先定義證據、候選與不可寫的內容。
  2. 把問題拆成欄位固定的小任務。
  3. AI 查公開資料，結果存成新檔。
  4. 人或另一個 AI 對照原頁抽查。
  5. 缺口標為 UNKNOWN，不補猜。
  6. 由人決定補查、換方法或暫緩。
  7. 整理成同一套樣式的成品。
  8. 換場景時沿用章節與題目，只換證據。
  9. 公開前去識別化，並決定哪些只留內部。
  10. 每次版本與決定留在檔案，之後可回溯。

06 · Limits

限制與尚未量測的部分

  • 這是方法研究，不是客戶交付；沒有客戶採用數據。
  • 未量測不用 AI 的基準時間，不主張節省時間或成本。
  • 公開的是方法；完整研究屬內部資料，不對外提供，公開頁刻意不含行業、地區、業者與金額細節。
  • 示範內容不代表任何市場或業者的結論。

Method demonstration · Human + AI

Turning a vague business question
into a decision-ready study

A real trade question served as the practice ground to show how I split work with several AIs, grade evidence and keep "unknown" as an honest answer. The subject and its conclusions are not published; the method is.

02 · Roles

What the person and AIs each do

A person sets the question, makes tradeoffs and signs off. AI findings still need checking against their sources.

Paul / decision-maker

Sets the question, decides tradeoffs and further checks, and signs off.

Does not treat AI conclusions as facts.

Codex

Uses task cards to research public pages and organise findings.

Does not contact people, estimate or guess domains.

GrokBot

Finds official texts, such as regulations and rules.

Does not make the commercial decision.

Claude Code

Writes task cards, spot-checks sources, records review notes and lays out the study.

Does not change governance originals or publish without approval.

03 · Evidence discipline

Separate facts, inferences and unknowns

FACT

Visible on the source page, with a URL and date.

INFERENCE

A judgment derived from facts, explicitly labelled as an inference.

UNKNOWN

When information is not found or a page cannot be opened, keep it unknown rather than asserting absence.

04 · Review

How review exposed gaps

These four examples come from the review record; they are not market outcomes.

01 Classification did not match reality

One candidate was labelled as qualifying; review opened its official pages and found the main product was something else, so its eligibility still needed confirmation.

02 An address is not a place of production

Another candidate's public address was an office and the page did not claim production; review relabelled it "candidate, production site unknown".

03 A rate sheet with a different purpose

A public rate sheet turned out to be project-specific rather than a general quote; review downgraded it to an order-of-magnitude reference.

04 Keeping the zero result

One follow-up round produced no qualifying rows and 18 documented rejections. The count comes from file counts, not working hours; no substitute sources were used to pad it.

05 · Process

Ten steps from question to deliverable

  1. Define evidence, candidate criteria and what must not be claimed.
  2. Break the question into small tasks with fixed fields.
  3. AI researches public sources and saves new files.
  4. A person or another AI spot-checks original pages.
  5. Mark gaps UNKNOWN rather than guessing.
  6. A person decides whether to dig further, change method or pause.
  7. Assemble the deliverable in one consistent layout.
  8. When the setting changes, reuse the chapters and questions and swap only the evidence.
  9. Anonymise before publishing and decide what stays internal.
  10. Keep every version and decision in files so it can be traced later.

06 · Limits

Limits and what was not measured

  • This is method research, not a client delivery; there is no customer adoption data.
  • No baseline without AI was measured, so no time or cost saving is claimed.
  • The method is public; the full study is internal and not shared, and this page deliberately omits industry, region, company and amount details.
  • The demonstration does not represent conclusions about any market or company.
