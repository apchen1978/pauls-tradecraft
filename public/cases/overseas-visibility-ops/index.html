2 · 這些錢買的是位子——哪一部分改由流程

錢買到的是位置：被人看到、拿到一份名單、或僱一個人去追。流程設計接得住的，是其中這一段：自己的露出、設計過的接觸與資格、以及哪裡必須留人。展覽上的握手、別人平台裡的排名，流程不假裝取代。名單好不好，也不在流程裡自動變好。

位子買到的流程可以接的部分流程不假裝取代
幾天的現場露出展覽結束後仍找得到的畫面、規格、網站或虛擬展間。網站就夠，VR 可選。攤位上的握手與當場看貨
別人平台上的曝光自己的站，買方不必先租你的位子才讀得懂產品該平台內部的排名與流量規則
一批聯絡方式接觸節奏、初篩協助、跟催提醒名單本身的品質
名錄上的一筆資料買方讀得懂的第一印象：規格、英文、站得住的露出名錄機構的背書
一個人天天去追草稿與提醒先備好，人把時間留在資格與談判人的判斷與例外

露出→對準→接觸→資格→交接
  1. 露出 — 產品畫面、規格、網站或虛擬展間。
  2. 對準 — 誰該看見，以及買方聽得懂的理由。相關不等於有採購路徑。
  3. 接觸 — 設計過的節奏。草稿可以先寫好；承諾不自動送出。
  4. 資格 — 人決定要不要報價、要不要繼續。缺的事實維持待確認。
  5. 交接 — 條件說清楚之後，才進商務決策工作台，問現在能不能承諾。

4 · 人 × AI 的邊界

機器可以做人必須留下
素材、草稿、結構資格：這個人、這家公司值不值得投入
篩選協助，標出仍待確認的點報價策略
跟催提醒付款與接受條件
不代人答應交期承諾、談判

AI 在流程裡面加速，不是身份。知道怎麼用 AI，不會因此自動冒出客戶。聊天機器人回得了句子，回不了這張單該不該報、款該怎麼收、貨能不能答應。

Overseas Visibility Ops | You don’t have to only buy a seat

Method demo · not yet live-market validated

Figures blank · none invented

Judgment stays terms, risk, and process. This page is before the commit. Overseas exposure is often a seat you pay for: a booth, a platform, a list, a directory, or a person hired to chase. That money buys a position. Part of the position can be process design. Many owners do not know the option exists. Knowing how is itself the valuable part.

Alibaba, Apollo, and Dun & Bradstreet name the kind of paid-position industry. They are not features this work plugs in, and not a roster we sell.

2 · The money buys a position — which part process can take

The fee buys being seen, a list of names, or a person whose job is chase. Process design can take this slice: your own exposure, a designed approach and qualification, and a line for what a person must still decide. It does not pretend to replace a handshake at a fair, a rank inside someone else’s platform, or the quality of a purchased list.

3 · DEMO · Method and artifacts

Show → aim → approach → a person qualifies → hand legible terms to the Commercial Decision Desk. Designed artifacts — an exposure draft, a contact rhythm, a handoff of terms — do not yet exist as a client-filled file. The MORI site shows a website can carry visuals and a next step. It does not show orders from this workflow. Label on all of it: method demo · not yet live-market validated.

4 · Human × AI

Machine: assets, drafts, structuring, screening assist, follow-up reminders. Human: qualification, pricing strategy, payment and acceptance, delivery promises, negotiation. AI speeds the workflow. It is not the identity, and it does not find customers by itself.

No guaranteed orders.

No guaranteed list quality. A purchased list does not become accurate because a process exists.
