1 · 舊的感覺，和新的感覺

FACT 在這裡是結構，不是報價。舊做法怎麼發生、新做法想換成什麼節奏。沒有金額，也不把「省下很多」寫成已經算過的數字。

舊 · 買位子，然後等

  • 展覽、平台年費、很貴的名單
  • 助理一個一個疊上去追
  • 貴、慢
  • 露出一段時間，然後等人來問

新 · 一直在，而且找得到人

  • 畫面、規格、網站或虛擬展間，露出可以一直在
  • 接觸是設計過的：找得到客戶，也找得到該找的人
  • 不必把位子一直買回來
  • 資格、報價、付款、接受與交期，仍由人承諾

省下來的，是一直買位子、一直等詢問的那種重。展覽上的握手、別人平台裡的排名，這套流程不假裝取代。知道可以不必只買位子，本身就是這頁要留下的判斷。

2 · DEMO · 方法與產出

方法演示 · 尚未真人／市場驗證

平台還在成形。下面是作業怎麼走，以及它設計上要留下的東西。這些是演示，不是客戶結案。

露出→對準→接觸→資格→交接
  1. 露出 — 產品畫面、規格、網站或虛擬展間。網站就夠；VR 可選。
  2. 對準 — 誰該看見，以及買方聽得懂的理由。相關不等於有採購路徑。
  3. 接觸 — 設計過的節奏，所以找得到人，而不是等人撞上門。草稿可以先寫好；承諾不自動送出。
  4. 資格 — 人決定要不要報價、要不要繼續。缺的事實維持待確認。
  5. 交接 — 條件說清楚之後，才進商務決策工作台，問現在能不能承諾。

3 · 人 × AI 的邊界

機器可以做人必須留下
素材、草稿、結構資格：這個人、這家公司值不值得投入
篩選協助，標出仍待確認的點報價策略
跟催提醒付款與接受條件
不代人答應交期承諾、談判

AI 在流程裡面加速，不是身份，也不是這套平台的產品名稱。知道怎麼用 AI，不會因此自動冒出客戶。聊天機器人回得了句子，回不了這張單該不該報、款該怎麼收、貨能不能答應。

4 · 姿態：連不該信的也講清楚

不保證訂單。找得到人，是這套作業被設計來做的事，不是已經驗證的成交。

不保證名單品質。買來的名單不會因為流程存在就變準。

不把「省下很多」寫成數字。感覺是那條路變輕，不是一張已結算的帳單。

方法演示 ≠ 客戶成果。這也不是一套可以訂閱的軟體產品。

會不會變成訂單，是 UNKNOWN。我們甚至會告訴你，現在還不該信這一段。

5 · 為什麼是 Paul

我同時看兩本帳。工廠這一側：MOQ、師傅、交期為什麼會爆。買方這一側：老外怎麼讀一家供應商、英文怎麼把第一印象站住、那印象算不算數。不是 AI 行銷人，也不是只負責把訂單簽回來的業務。總監級的判斷還是條款、風險與流程；平台是把這個判斷前面的露出與接觸做出來。

1 · Old feeling, new feeling

The FACT here is structural, not a quote. How the old mode behaves, and the rhythm the new mode is designed to replace. No amounts. “A lot lighter” is not a calculated saving.

Old · Buy a seat, then wait

  • Fairs, platform fees, expensive lists
  • Assistants stacked on to chase
  • Expensive, slow
  • Exposure for a while, then wait for inquiries

New · Stay on, and find people

  • Visuals, specs, a site or virtual showroom — visibility can stay on
  • Reach is designed: find customers, and the right people
  • Stop buying the seat back every time
  • Qualification, price, payment, acceptance, and delivery promises stay human

What gets lighter is the habit of buying a seat and waiting to be asked. A handshake at a fair, or a rank inside someone else’s platform, is not something this process pretends to replace. Knowing you do not have to only buy the seat is the judgment this page leaves behind.

2 · DEMO · Method and artifacts

Show → aim → approach → a person qualifies → hand legible terms to the Commercial Decision Desk. Designed artifacts — an exposure draft, a contact rhythm, a handoff of terms — do not yet exist as a client-filled file. The MORI site shows a website can carry visuals and a next step. It does not show orders from this workflow. Label: method demo · not yet live-market validated. The platform is still in formation.

3 · Human × AI

Machine: assets, drafts, structuring, screening assist, follow-up reminders. Human: qualification, pricing strategy, payment and acceptance, delivery promises, negotiation. AI speeds the workflow. It is not the identity, and it does not find customers by itself.

4 · Posture — including what not to trust

No guaranteed orders. Finding people is what the workflow is designed to do. It is not a proven close.

No guaranteed list quality.

No dollar figure on the saving. The feeling is that the path gets lighter, not that a bill has been settled.

Demo ≠ client results. This is also not a software product you subscribe to.

Whether this becomes orders is UNKNOWN. That is the part you should not trust yet.

5 · Why Paul

Both ledgers. Factory side: MOQ, the craftspeople, and why a lead time blows up. Buyer side: how a foreign buyer reads a supplier, how English carries the first impression, and whether that impression is credible. Not an AI marketer. Not a salesperson whose only job is to bring the order home. Director-level judgment is still terms, risk, and process. The platform is how the visibility and the reach in front of that judgment get made.
