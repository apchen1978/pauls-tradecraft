One product · Four stations

帶一項產品，
走到第一櫃

負責人拿出一項產品。Paul 不先談訂單，先走四站：選路、選人、能不能承諾、算得過嗎。每一站只回答一件事：現在要決定什麼，以及他的判斷從哪裡進來。

示範／成效未驗證 · 公司與情境皆為虛構

這份虛構條件

整條故事線只用下面這一組數字。公司是編的，沒有接觸過任何人。四個公開示範仍是分開的原型：這頁把它們讀成同一條故事線，不讓它們交換資料，也不改寫它們既有的案例。

產品

美式純紙牆紙，可訂製。卷規格 0.686 m × 8.23 m（約 5.65 m²）。FOB 上海每卷 USD 10–15。

要找誰

美國與歐洲的進口商，以及大型設計公司。希望第一張單是一個 20 呎櫃。

起訂

MOQ 1,000 直米。以卷長 8.23 公尺估算，約 122 卷。卷價、直米和櫃子是不同單位，不能直接當成同一件事。

貨櫃

一個 20 呎櫃大約 3,000–5,000 卷，貨值大約 USD 30,000–75,000。估算，待工廠確認。這不是營收，也不是已成交的金額。

01 · 選路

先決定走哪一條路

海外商業開發

這一站要決定什麼

同一項產品，先走進口商，還是大型設計公司？目標若是「第一張單就是一個櫃子」，和「先試訂製」，優先順序不一樣。

  • 進口商比較接近可重複的採購節奏，較有機會把首單放到一個櫃子。
  • 大型設計公司比較像在試訂製：單案量小，全年可能有多個專案，不宜把想像中的案子加總成一個櫃子。

Paul 的判斷

純紙牆紙達不到酒店與商用規格（對方要的是商用乙烯壁布，加上防火等級）。那一整段先擱下，不排進首輪。

要試的角度是小批量可訂製，不是「一定更便宜」。Paul 看到的歐美工廠大約在 FOB 每卷 USD 12–25、起訂量較高；這不是已核實的行情。USD 10–15 和 USD 12–25 有重疊，不能說我們一定比較便宜。

這一站公開示範裡，還沒有這份牆紙。現有的海外商業開發示範是另一個虛構情境：切換負責人的目標，看下一步研究順序怎麼變。它不是海灣地區酒店那份案例。兩份示範並排留著，這頁不改寫它。

02 · 選人

名單出來之後，人怎麼挑

海外客戶開發

這一站要決定什麼

不是照名單順序往下做。先看產品能說什麼、不能說什麼，再留下少數值得研究的對象，寫下為什麼是它、第一次接觸的角度，以及還要問的問題。

Paul 的判斷

這一站已經寫好，用的就是上面這份牆紙。三家入選、三家暫緩，理由不一樣。

  • 入選：Kensington Pattern Co.、Harbor & Vine Wallcoverings、Redwood Line Design。都是虛構公司。
  • 暫緩：Lumen Studio Group、Bayview Hospitality Design（酒店／商用規格與純紙不合）、Atlas Home Supply（價格與付款和接受線不合）。

接手頁寫的是接觸前的判斷，不是開發信，也沒有寄出任何訊息。它不是名單示範的「後續成果」。

03 · 能不能承諾

假設詢問來了，現在能不能答應

商務決策工作台

這一站要決定什麼

為了讓這一站有東西可判斷，假設 Kensington Pattern Co.（虛構）提出一筆詢問：純紙牆紙、可否訂製、首單能否朝一個 20 呎櫃。沒有真實接觸，也沒有信件。

還沒有答案、不能補成事實的事：

  • 卷規格是否就是 0.686 m × 8.23 m
  • 一個櫃子實際裝得下多少
  • 認證要求
  • 打樣與交期
  • 對方要的付款條件

Paul 的接受線

對這筆虛構詢問，付款他願意放上桌的是：訂金 30%，餘款出貨前付清。對方實際要什麼，仍是未知；這條線不是已經談成的條件。

卷規格、裝櫃、認證、打樣與交期還沒核對之前，不把「可以訂製」或「可以一個櫃子」說成承諾。

工作台公開示範裡，還沒有這份牆紙。打開它，看到的仍是另一個虛構案例：海灣地區的酒店。那份案例留在原處，和這條故事線並排。這頁不把牆紙數字寫進那個示範，也不改寫海灣案例。

04 · 算得過嗎

價錢與數量，有沒有過得了線

貿易利潤導航

這一站要決定什麼

FOB 上海每卷 USD 10–15、MOQ 1,000 直米（約 122 卷）、目標一個 20 呎櫃。貨櫃大約 3,000–5,000 卷，貨值大約 USD 30,000–75,000。估算，待工廠確認。

這頁沒有把這些數字算成毛利。毛利率大約 35% 是 Paul 對這份虛構情境畫的地板：守不住這條線，價格就不算可談。它不是算出來的結果，也不是已發生的成效。

Paul 的地板

最低毛利率大約 35%。下面三欄是他對這份虛構條件的記號，不是報價單。

可談

  • 售價落在 FOB 上海每卷 USD 10–15，而且毛利率仍守得住大約 35%。
  • 以 1,000 直米起訂、小批量可訂製作為接觸角度。

先查

  • 卷規格 0.686 m × 8.23 m，以及 1,000 直米約等於 122 卷，要跟工廠的實際卷長核對。
  • 裝櫃量與貨值（估算，待工廠確認）。
  • 認證、打樣、交期，以及對方付款是否落在訂金 30%、餘款出貨前付清。

不接受

  • 用純紙去接需要商用乙烯壁布與防火等級的酒店／商用規格。
  • 說成一定比歐美廠便宜。兩邊價格區間有重疊。
  • 工廠還沒確認裝櫃量，就把整櫃數量寫成可以承諾的數字。
  • 比「訂金 30%、餘款出貨前付清」更寬的付款。

利潤導航的公開示範裡，還沒有這份牆紙。打開它，用的仍是海灣酒店案例的數字。那份案例留在原處。這頁的 35% 與牆紙價格沒有放進那個示範，也不把它的計算結果說成這項產品的毛利。

打開利潤導航示範（海灣案例） 回作品集的這一項

四站留下的是判斷，不是訂單

選路先擱下酒店／商用那一段；選人留下三家入選、三家暫緩；承諾前先標出卷規格、裝櫃、認證、打樣交期與付款；算不算得過，則以大約 35% 毛利率為地板，並標成可談、先查或不接受。

四件作品仍是各自獨立的原型，由這條故事線串在一起。它們不交換資料，也不共用資料模型。示範／成效未驗證。

One product · Four stations

From one product
to the first container

A decision-maker brings one product. Paul does not start with an order. He walks four stations: which path, which people, whether a commitment is possible, and whether the numbers can hold. Each station answers one thing: what has to be decided, and where his judgment enters.

The fictional conditions

The whole storyline uses only the figures below. The companies are invented. Nobody has been contacted. The four public demos remain separate prototypes. This page reads them as one storyline. It does not make them exchange data, and it does not rewrite the cases they already show.

Product

American-style pure-paper wallpaper. Customization is possible. Roll size 0.686 m × 8.23 m (about 5.65 m²). FOB Shanghai USD 10–15 per roll.

Who to study

Importers and large design firms in the US and Europe. The hope for a first order is one 20-foot container.

Minimum

MOQ 1,000 linear meters (LM). At a roll length of 8.23 m, that is about 122 rolls. Price per roll, linear meters, and a container are different units. They are not the same fact.

Container

One 20-foot container is roughly 3,000–5,000 rolls, about USD 30,000–75,000 of goods. Estimate, to be confirmed by the factory. This is not revenue, and it is not a completed sale.

01 · Choose the path

Which path comes first

Global Business Development

What this station decides

For the same product, do we study importers first, or large design firms? “The first order should be one container” and “test customization first” do not produce the same priority.

  • Importers sit closer to a repeatable buying rhythm, and closer to a first order that could fill one container.
  • Large design firms are more often a customization test: small projects, possibly several in a year. Imagined projects are not added up into a container.

Paul’s call

Pure paper will not meet hotel and commercial specifications. Those projects ask for contract vinyl and a fire rating. That whole segment is set aside first.

The angle to test is customization at a smaller batch, not “always cheaper.” In Paul’s view, US and European mills sit roughly at USD 12–25 per roll FOB, with higher MOQs. That is not verified market pricing. USD 10–15 and USD 12–25 overlap, so a lower price is not a claim he will make.

This station does not yet have a wallpaper demo. The public Global Business Development demo is a different fictional scenario: switch the decision-maker’s objective and watch the next research priority change. It is not the Gulf-region hotel case. The two sit side by side. This page does not rewrite that demo.

02 · Choose the people

After a list, a person still chooses

Overseas Lead Discovery

What this station decides

Not working down the list in order. First, what the product can and cannot credibly say. Then a few accounts worth studying, each with a reason, a first-contact angle, and the questions still open.

Paul’s call

This station is already written, on the wallpaper conditions above. Three accounts are picked and three are deferred, for different reasons.

  • Picked: Kensington Pattern Co., Harbor & Vine Wallcoverings, Redwood Line Design. All fictional.
  • Deferred: Lumen Studio Group and Bayview Hospitality Design (hotel and commercial specs do not match pure paper), and Atlas Home Supply (price and payment sit outside the line he would accept).

The takeover page is a judgment before contact. It is not an outreach letter, and nothing has been sent. It is not an outcome of the list demo.

03 · Can we commit?

A fictional enquiry, and what is still unknown

Commercial Decision Desk

What this station decides

So this station has something to judge, assume a fictional enquiry from Kensington Pattern Co.: pure-paper wallpaper, whether customization is possible, and whether a first order could move toward one 20-foot container. There has been no real contact, and there is no letter.

Still unknown, and not filled in as fact:

  • Whether the roll really is 0.686 m × 8.23 m
  • How many rolls a container actually holds
  • Certification
  • Sampling and lead time
  • The payment terms the other side wants

Paul’s acceptance line

On this fictional enquiry, the payment line he will put on the table is a 30% deposit, with the balance paid before shipment. What the other side actually wants is still unknown. The line is not an agreed term.

Until roll spec, container loading, certification, sampling, and lead time are checked, “we can customize” and “we can fill a container” are not commitments.

The public desk demo does not yet use this wallpaper. Open it and the sample is still a separate fictional case: a Gulf-region hotel. That case stays where it is, beside this storyline. This page does not load the wallpaper figures into that demo, and it does not rewrite the Gulf case.

04 · Do the numbers hold?

Price, quantity, and a floor

Trade Profit Navigator

What this station decides

FOB Shanghai USD 10–15 per roll, MOQ 1,000 linear meters (LM, about 122 rolls), and a first order aimed at one 20-foot container. The container is roughly 3,000–5,000 rolls, about USD 30,000–75,000 of goods. Estimate, to be confirmed by the factory.

This page does not calculate a margin from those figures. About 35% gross margin is the floor Paul draws for this fictional case. If the price cannot stay on that side of the line, he does not mark it as something to discuss. It is not a computed result, and it is not an outcome that has occurred.

Paul’s floor

Minimum gross margin about 35%. The three marks below are his notes on this fictional case, not a quotation.

Can discuss（可談）

  • A price inside USD 10–15 per roll FOB Shanghai, while gross margin stays at or above about 35%.
  • Leading with customization at an MOQ of 1,000 linear meters (LM).

Check first（先查）

  • Roll size 0.686 m × 8.23 m, and whether 1,000 linear meters (LM) is really about 122 rolls, against the factory’s actual roll length.
  • Container loading and goods value (estimate, to be confirmed by the factory).
  • Certification, sampling, lead time, and whether payment is a 30% deposit with the balance before shipment.

Not accepted（不接受）

  • Offering pure paper where the specification needs contract vinyl and a fire rating.
  • Saying the price is always lower than US and European mills. The ranges overlap.
  • Writing a container quantity as a commitment before the factory confirms loading.
  • Payment wider than a 30% deposit and the balance before shipment.

The public Profit Navigator demo does not yet use this wallpaper. Open it and the figures are still the Gulf hotel case. That case stays where it is. The 35% floor and the wallpaper prices on this page are not loaded into that demo, and its calculation is not presented as this product’s margin.

Four stations leave a judgment, not an order

The path sets hotel and commercial work aside. The people station keeps three accounts and defers three.
