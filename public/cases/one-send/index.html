CASE · ONE SEND

只寄一次：四個設計選擇

這是一封虛構的報價信。八句話，信封只裝得下三句，寄出就不能改。下面是這件作品的四個設計選擇，以及背後的原因。

示範情境 · 不預測買家反應 只記在這個瀏覽器
寫一封只寄一次的信 聊聊你的報價信

四個設計選擇

1. 產品名稱要像真的買得到

選擇
用一個溫暖、具體的日常產品，而不是中性的佔位詞。
原因
讀的人要先相信這封信是真的，留下哪三句才會是認真的選擇。

2. 不提供重來的連結

選擇
寄出之後，沒有重設或重玩的入口。
原因
能重來，「只寄一次」就不成立了。

3. 八句話有強有弱

選擇
八句刻意寫得有強有弱，而不是每一句都一樣好。
原因
句子太平均，選的時候不會猶豫；沒有猶豫，就看不出一個人的判斷。

4. 收尾只說一句

選擇
選完三句之後，只留下一句：「三句留下來，也決定了買家先看見哪一面的你。」
原因
選擇本身已經有分量，收尾不需要再解釋，只需要點出這三句的意義。

只能寄一次，每一句才需要被認真挑選。

這頁不主張

買家是虛構的，信是虛構的。沒有任何買家看過、回覆過這封信，這頁不說明它有沒有效果。

這頁只說明這件作品的設計選擇，不代表同樣的做法適用於你的產品或市場。

CASE · ONE SEND

One Send: four design choices

This is a fictional quotation letter. Eight sentences, an envelope that holds only three, and no edits after sending. Below are the four design choices behind the piece, and the reasons for each.

Four design choices

1. A product name that feels real

Choice
Use a warm, concrete everyday product rather than a neutral placeholder.
Why
The reader has to believe the letter is real before choosing three sentences feels like a serious choice.

2. No way to start over

Choice
Once the letter is sent, there is no reset or play-again link.
Why
If you can start over, "send once" is no longer true.

3. Strong and weak sentences

Choice
The eight sentences are deliberately uneven, rather than all equally good.
Why
When every sentence is equally good, choosing never makes you hesitate, and without hesitation there is no judgment to see.

4. One closing line

Choice
After you choose three sentences, the page says just one thing: "The three sentences you kept shape the buyer's first impression of you."
Why
The choice already carries weight. The closing line does not need to explain it, only to name what the three sentences mean.

When a letter can be sent only once, every sentence has to earn its place.

What this page does not claim

The buyer and the letter are fictional. No buyer has seen or answered this letter, and this page says nothing about whether it works.

It explains the design choices in this piece only. It does not say the same approach fits your product or market.
