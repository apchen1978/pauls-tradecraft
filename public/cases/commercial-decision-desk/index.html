CASE · COMMERCIAL DECISION

訂單看起來不錯——現在能不能承諾？

商務決策工作台（CDD）把報價、付款、交付與責任條件，連同 UNKNOWN，整理成可帶進會議的 Deal Brief。AI 協助結構化比較；人負責授權與最終承諾。

引擎：技術驗證 · 案例：合成
打開 CDD sample 帶一個真實交易問題來談

Decision question

這筆單，現在能不能承諾？

Input

RFQ／報價條件／付款與交期（可匿名或合成）

Output

Decision Path + Deal Brief（已知／UNKNOWN／下一步）

Human gate

Owner／業務主管授權；系統不自動對外承諾

情境

出口與 B2B 場景裡，單看似「毛利不錯」常被直接推進。真正卡住的是：付款何時進來、工廠何時要錢、交付與違約責任誰控、哪些事實仍是 UNKNOWN。CDD 把這些攤開，而不是用摘要掩蓋。

方法（對齊 PAUL OS）

  1. Intake — 收斂交易問題與可用輸入
  2. Structure terms — 付款、交付、責任、數量／價格結構
  3. Surface UNKNOWN — 缺資料不補成事實
  4. Decision states — 可推進／有條件／暫緩／拒絕（依系統定義）
  5. Human commit — 人做最終承諾；留下可交接產物

ENGLISH SUMMARY

Commercial Decision Desk — can we commit now?

engine: technically validated · case: synthetic

CDD structures payment, delivery, liability, and UNKNOWNs into a Deal Brief a team can take into a meeting. AI widens comparison; humans keep authorization and the final commitment.

Fact: the public sample can be inspected interactively.
Design or usefulness claim, not a fact: UNKNOWN is not auto-filled into fact; the output is intended as a meeting handoff.
Not verified: win rate, revenue, ROI, cross-industry validity, or replacement of legal/credit diligence.

These are independent prototypes that share a scenario concept; they do not exchange data or share a data model.
