01

先定義要找誰Define

不是先找 EmailStart with a buyer hypothesis

什麼樣的公司可能會買？Who might actually buy?

先想市場、產品用途、買方類型、公司型態和商業模式，再開始找名單。Set the market, application, buyer type, company shape, and business model before searching for names.

這一步留下Output目標客戶假設Target customer profile
看可能的買方與判斷線索Possible buyers and signals

可能的買方Possible buyer organizations

進口商、經銷商、批發商、品牌商、OEM／ODM 買家、承包商、專案規格單位或通路夥伴。Importers, distributors, wholesalers, brands, OEM / ODM buyers, contractors, specifiers, or channel partners.

商業判斷Commercial judgment

先形成「誰可能買、為什麼」的假設。不同產品、應用與市場，目標買方都可能不同。Form a hypothesis about who may buy and why. Buyer types vary by product, application, and market.

從公開網頁、地圖、產業名錄、展會、商會與公司情報來源找候選公司。Find candidate companies across public web, maps, directories, trade fairs, associations, and company-intelligence sources.

可能的來源Possible sources

公開網頁與搜尋、地圖與地方商業資料、產業名錄、展商與協會名單、商業公司情報。Public web and search, maps and local business data, directories, exhibitor and association lists, and commercial company intelligence.

AI / Agents 可協助AI / agents can assist with

研究、擷取、格式整理、分類、去重與保留來源。實際來源與工具依情境選擇，不預設特定供應商。Research, extraction, normalization, classification, deduplication, and evidence capture. Sources and tools depend on the case; no vendor is assumed.

找到 1,000 家，不代表有 1,000 個潛在客戶。Finding 1,000 companies does not create 1,000 prospects.

03

挑出值得研究的公司Qualify

不是每家公司都值得追Evidence before attention

哪些公司值得多花一點時間？Which companies deserve a closer look?

比較買方、品類、市場、商業模式與進口線索；證據不夠的地方就標為未知。Compare buyer, category, market, business model, and import signals. Keep gaps marked unknown.

看證據切面與 R8 反例Evidence cuts and the R8 counterexample

值得檢查什麼What to check

買方適配、品類適配、市場適配、商業模式、進口與配送證據、相關產品及證據品質。Buyer, category, and market fit; business model; import and distribution evidence; relevant products; and evidence quality.

既有研究中的三個證據切面Three evidence views in the existing research

44 家發現的候選公司；candidates discovered; 20 家通過證據篩選；evidence-qualified shortlist; 8 筆匿名示例紀錄供比較。它們不是同一批逐層轉換的漏斗。anonymized records for comparison. These are different evidence cuts, not a stage-by-stage conversion funnel.

R8

買方類型吻合／有進口意願／產品品類不合 → 暫不優先BUYER FIT HIGH / IMPORT OPENNESS YES / CATEGORY FIT WEAK → NOT PRIORITIZED
兩項正面訊號仍不足以蓋過品類不適配。找到公司，不等於值得投入業務時間。Two positive signals do not outweigh weak category fit. Finding a company does not mean it deserves sales time.

不只找一個 EmailRole before contact details

誰能推動這個機會？Who inside can move the opportunity?

從公司型態和商業模式，推想採購功能、可能職務，再找合適的接觸路徑。Reason from company type and business model to buying function, likely role, and a suitable contact path.

展開角色與聯絡路徑See the role and contact reasoning

推理路徑Reasoning path

公司型態 → 商業模式 → 採購功能 → 可能職務 → 接觸路徑Company type → business model → buying function → likely role → contact path

可從公司官網、公開網頁、專業人脈與公司情報交叉查找；聯絡人探索及商務信箱檢核屬可替換的能力，找到資料仍要核實。Company sites, public-web evidence, professional networks, and company intelligence can be cross-checked. Contact discovery and business-email verification are swappable capabilities; any result still needs checking.

可能職務Possible roles

創辦人、品類經理、產品經理、採購或 sourcing、進口經理、商務主管、專案規格角色。職務不會在每間公司都相同。Founder, category or product manager, purchasing or sourcing, import manager, commercial lead, or project specifier. Roles vary by organization.

05

帶著相關內容接觸Engage

讓對方有理由回應Make the conversation relevant

為什麼對方要停下來看？Why should this person pay attention?

依對象、角色與情境，準備合適的介紹、產品資料、應用提案，再選擇接觸管道。Match the message, product information, and application idea to the person and context, then choose the channel.

這一步留下Output準備好的接觸內容Relevant material ready for review
看接觸管道與準備內容Channels and materials

可能的管道Possible channels

Email、專業人脈、適當的商務訊息、展後跟進、轉介、公司網站或影片內容。Email, professional networks, appropriate business messages, trade-fair follow-up, referrals, owned websites, or video.

可能的準備Possible preparation

個人化介紹、一頁式數位簡報、產品資料、證據包、樣品提案或相關應用案例。品類經理與公司負責人，不應自動收到同一套說法。A tailored introduction, one-page digital brief, product sheet, evidence pack, sample proposal, or relevant application. A category manager and an owner should not automatically receive the same message.

AI-native 客戶開發，不等於自動寄陌生開發信。AI-native customer development is not cold-email automation.

06

讀懂買方回覆Convert

意向出現，仍要人確認Intent is a signal, not a commitment

對方的回覆代表什麼？What does the buyer's reply mean?

辨認目錄、價格、MOQ、樣品、客製、交期或報價需求，再判斷是否形成商業機會。Recognize requests for catalogs, pricing, MOQ, samples, customization, lead time, or a quote, then assess whether an opportunity is forming.

這一步留下Output詢價或待確認商機RFQ or opportunity to review
看 AI 輔助與商機狀態轉換AI assistance and the state change

回覆裡可能出現Possible buyer replies

請寄型錄、請報價、MOQ 是多少、能否客製、寄樣品、下季再聯繫、找錯人，或請提供特定數量與條件的報價。Send a catalog, quote this, what is your MOQ, can you customize, send samples, contact next quarter, wrong person, or quote a specified quantity and terms.

可能的 AI 協助Possible AI assistance

分類回覆、擷取數量與條件、整理摘要、草擬後續處理方向。人確認意向及狀態，不能把訊號當成承諾。Classify the reply, extract quantities and terms, summarize it, and draft a possible next action. A person confirms intent and status; a signal is not a commitment.

07

判斷能不能接、怎麼交易Decide and trade

商業承諾由人負責People own the commitment

公司實際上能接這筆生意嗎？Can the business actually take this order?

檢查價格、MOQ、毛利、產能、交期、付款、信用風險、客製需求、證據缺口與貿易條件。Check price, MOQ, margin, capacity, lead time, payment, credit exposure, customization, evidence gaps, and trade terms.

這一步留下Output報價、暫緩、補證據或婉拒Quote, hold, verify, or decline
看可能的商業動作與既有工具Possible actions and existing work

下一步選擇Possible decisions

報價、暫緩、升級討論、婉拒，或要求更多證據。Incoterms 是交易條件的一部分，須按實際交易核實。Quote, hold, escalate, decline, or request more evidence. Incoterms form part of trade terms and must be checked against the actual transaction.

商業判斷Commercial judgment

AI 可以整理資訊與選項，但價格、信用、產能、付款、交期、風險接受與商業承諾由有權責的人決定。AI can organize information and options. Authorized people decide price, credit, capacity, payment, lead time, risk acceptance, and commercial commitments.
