海外客戶開發Overseas customer development

找海外客戶，可以一步一步來。How overseas customers can be found, step by step.

先確認公司真正能提供什麼，再看哪些市場和買家可能需要它。這張路線圖帶你從找公司、找對口的人，走到解讀詢價和決定下一步。Start with what the company can credibly offer, then explore which markets and buyers may need it. Follow the route from finding companies and buyer roles to reading an inquiry and choosing a next move.

從能提供什麼，到下一個商業行動From supplier reality to a next commercial move

供應能力Capability→市場Market→公司Accounts→對的人People→詢價Inquiry→交易判斷Trade decision

展會、平台、轉介和既有經銷商都能帶來線索。現在也能用 AI 查市場、找公司、整理資料；跟買家建立關係，還是得靠人。Alibaba, trade fairs, referrals, and distributors can all be starting points. New research and AI capabilities connect the work around them; they do not replace relationships.

找到公司，只是第一步。Finding a company is not finding an opportunity.

AI 和資料工具可以幫忙查公司、整理線索、準備聯絡內容。要不要追、怎麼談、能不能接單，還是由人判斷。Data and AI can assist research, organization, and preparation. Commercial judgment still decides where to invest attention, how to engage, and what the business can commit to.

開發海外客戶，要回答七個問題One route, seven commercial questions

每一步都會說明要回答什麼、要做什麼、會留下什麼。想知道資料和 AI 怎麼幫忙，再展開細節。Each step shows the question, the work, and what it produces. Open the details to see where data, AI, and people contribute.

開始前Before step 01

我們真正能提供什麼？What can we credibly offer?

先核對產品、可交付能力與限制，再說清這次開發想驗證什麼。尚未確認的規格、MOQ、產能或交期，不能拿來對買家承諾。Check the product, delivery capability, and constraints, then state what this commercial effort aims to test. Unconfirmed specifications, MOQ, capacity, or lead time cannot become buyer promises.

01

先說清楚要找哪種公司Define

先想買家，再找公司Start with a buyer hypothesis

哪些公司可能需要這類產品？Who might actually buy?

帶著已知的供應能力與限制，選市場，推想產品用途、買方類型和採購路徑，再開始找公司。Use known supplier capabilities and constraints to choose a market and form hypotheses about product use, buyer type, and buying path before searching for names.

這一步留下Output市場與買方假設Market and buyer hypothesis
看可能的買方與判斷線索Possible buyers and signals

可能的買方Possible buyer organizations

進口商、經銷商、批發商、品牌商、OEM／ODM 買家、承包商、專案規格單位或通路夥伴。Importers, distributors, wholesalers, brands, OEM / ODM buyers, contractors, specifiers, or channel partners.

商業判斷Commercial judgment

先形成「誰可能買、為什麼」的假設。不同產品、應用與市場，目標買方都可能不同。Form a hypothesis about who may buy and why. Buyer types vary by product, application, and market.

02

找到可能的公司Discover

名單只是起點Sources create candidates

這些公司可能在哪裡找到？Where can those companies be found?

從公司網站、地圖、產業名錄、展會和商會名單，找出可能相關的公司。Find candidate companies across public web, maps, directories, trade fairs, associations, and company-intelligence sources.

這一步留下Output找到的公司名單Candidate account pool
看資料與 AI 可以怎麼幫忙How data and AI can help

可能的來源Possible sources

公開網頁與搜尋、地圖與地方商業資料、產業名錄、展商與協會名單、商業公司情報。Public web and search, maps and local business data, directories, exhibitor and association lists, and commercial company intelligence.

AI / Agents 可協助AI / agents can assist with

研究、擷取、格式整理、分類、去重與保留來源。實際來源與工具依情境選擇，不預設特定供應商。Research, extraction, normalization, classification, deduplication, and evidence capture. Sources and tools depend on the case; no vendor is assumed.

找到 1,000 家，不代表有 1,000 個潛在客戶。Finding 1,000 companies does not create 1,000 prospects.

03

留下值得查的公司Qualify

先看證據，再花時間Evidence before attention

哪些公司值得業務多看一眼？Which companies deserve a closer look?

比對公司類型、產品類別、市場和進口線索；找不到證據的地方，就先標成未知。Compare buyer, category, market, business model, and import signals. Keep gaps marked unknown.

值得檢查什麼What to check

公司類型和產品是否合適、怎麼做生意、市場和進口線索、相關產品，以及資料是否可靠。Buyer, category, and market fit; business model; import and distribution evidence; relevant products; and evidence quality.

既有研究中的三種資料整理結果Three evidence views in the existing research

44 家找到的候選公司；candidates discovered; 20 家符合進一步查核條件；evidence-qualified shortlist; 8 筆匿名紀錄供比較。三個數字是不同資料切面，不是同一批公司逐層轉換的漏斗，也不是成效數字。anonymized records for comparison. These are different evidence cuts, not a stage-by-stage conversion funnel.

R8

買方類型吻合／市場有進口空間／產品品類不合 → 暫不優先BUYER FIT HIGH / IMPORT OPENNESS YES / CATEGORY FIT WEAK → NOT PRIORITIZED
買方類型和市場條件看來合適，但產品品類不合，所以不優先花業務時間。Two positive signals do not outweigh weak category fit. Finding a company does not mean it deserves sales time.

04

找出公司裡負責這件事的人Identify

職務因公司而異Role before contact details

誰負責這類產品的採購？Who inside can move the opportunity?

先看公司怎麼做生意，再找可能負責選品、採購或進口的人。實際職務和權限還要確認。Reason from company type and business model to buying function, likely role, and a suitable contact path.

這一步留下Output可能對口的人，待確認A suitable account and a buying-role hypothesis
展開角色與聯絡路徑See the role and contact reasoning

推理路徑Reasoning path

公司類型 → 怎麼做生意 → 誰負責採購 → 聯絡方式Company type → business model → buying function → likely role → contact path

可從公司官網、公開網頁、專業人脈與公司情報交叉查找；聯絡人探索及商務信箱檢核屬可替換的能力，找到資料仍要核實。Company sites, public-web evidence, professional networks, and company intelligence can be cross-checked. Contact discovery and business-email verification are swappable capabilities; any result still needs checking.

可能職務Possible roles

可能是創辦人、產品或品類經理、採購、進口或商務主管。每家公司分工不同，實際職務仍要確認。Founder, category or product manager, purchasing or sourcing, import manager, commercial lead, or project specifier. Roles vary by organization.

05

準備好再聯絡Engage

說清楚為什麼值得看Make the conversation relevant

怎麼讓對方願意回應？Why should this person pay attention?

依買家、職務和情境，準備合適的介紹、產品資料或應用提案，再選擇聯絡方式。Match the message, product information, and application idea to the person and context, then choose the channel.

這一步留下Output待人檢查的聯絡內容Relevant material ready for review
看接觸管道與準備內容Channels and materials

可能的管道Possible channels

Email、專業人脈、適當的商務訊息、展後跟進、轉介、公司網站或影片內容。Email, professional networks, appropriate business messages, trade-fair follow-up, referrals, owned websites, or video.

可能的準備Possible preparation

個人化介紹、一頁式數位簡報、產品資料、證據包、樣品提案或相關應用案例。品類經理與公司負責人，不應自動收到同一套說法。A tailored introduction, one-page digital brief, product sheet, evidence pack, sample proposal, or relevant application. A category manager and an owner should not automatically receive the same message.

AI 可以幫忙查資料和準備內容，但不會替你自動寄開發信。AI-native customer development is not cold-email automation.

06

看懂買家問了什麼Convert

有興趣，不等於答應下單Intent is a signal, not a commitment

買家的回覆，接下來怎麼處理？What does the buyer's reply mean?

看看對方要型錄、報價、MOQ、樣品、客製還是交期，再判斷值不值得繼續談。Recognize requests for catalogs, pricing, MOQ, samples, customization, lead time, or a quote, then assess whether an opportunity is forming.

這一步留下Output待判斷的詢價或商機RFQ or opportunity to review
看 AI 輔助與商機狀態轉換AI assistance and the state change

回覆裡可能出現Possible buyer replies

請寄型錄、請報價、MOQ 是多少、能否客製、寄樣品、下季再聯繫、找錯人，或請提供特定數量與條件的報價。Send a catalog, quote this, what is your MOQ, can you customize, send samples, contact next quarter, wrong person, or quote a specified quantity and terms.

可能的 AI 協助Possible AI assistance

分類回覆、擷取數量與條件、整理摘要、草擬後續處理方向。人確認意向及狀態，不能把訊號當成承諾。Classify the reply, extract quantities and terms, summarize it, and draft a possible next action. A person confirms intent and status; a signal is not a commitment.

07

判斷這筆生意能不能接Decide and trade

報價和承諾由人負責People own the commitment

價格、產能和條件都能答應嗎？Can the business actually take this order?

核對價格、MOQ、毛利、產能、交期、付款、信用風險、客製要求和貿易條件。資料不足時先查清楚。Check price, MOQ, margin, capacity, lead time, payment, credit exposure, customization, evidence gaps, and trade terms.

這一步留下Output報價、補資料、暫緩或婉拒Quote, hold, verify, or decline
看可能的商業動作與既有工具Possible actions and existing work

下一步選擇Possible decisions

報價、暫緩、升級討論、婉拒，或要求更多證據。Incoterms 是交易條件的一部分，須按實際交易核實。Quote, hold, escalate, decline, or request more evidence. Incoterms form part of trade terms and must be checked against the actual transaction.

商業判斷Commercial judgment

AI 可以整理資訊與選項，但價格、信用、產能、付款、交期、風險接受與商業承諾由有權責的人決定。AI can organize information and options. Authorized people decide price, credit, capacity, payment, lead time, risk acceptance, and commercial commitments.

先決定下一步，再開始下一輪Choose the next move, then learn from it

資料不足時先 HOLD，查一項關鍵缺口；理由足夠時，由人選一個可撤回的小行動，例如確認買家角色或澄清需求。再用實際回覆修正下一輪要找的對象。If evidence is thin, hold and check one critical gap. When the case is strong enough, a person can choose a small reversible move, such as confirming the buyer role or clarifying the inquiry. Use observed responses to refine the next cycle.

補證據／小幅驗證／HOLD → 下一輪Check evidence / small test / HOLD → next cycle

每一步，都有兩條橫向能力Two capabilities run across the work

工具可以更換；資料、AI 與人的分工要清楚。Tools can change. The roles of data, AI, and people should remain clear.

AI 協助查找與準備AI assists and prepares

可按需要用 AI 查公司、補線索、準備內容和整理回覆；這些是可組合的能力，不代表已串成自動化流程。AI can support research, enrichment, preparation, and reply review as needed; these are composable capabilities, not a claim of an integrated automated workflow.

人決定先看哪個市場、投入哪些公司、如何接觸與回應，並為交易條件和承諾負責。People prioritize markets and accounts, choose how to engage, and own trade terms and commitments.
