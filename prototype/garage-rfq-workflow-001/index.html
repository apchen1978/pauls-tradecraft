RFQ → Quote Decision: Human × AI Workflow

See how an incomplete foreign-trade RFQ is reconstructed into evidence, judged by a person, and returned to review when new information changes the case.

Built and operationally red-teamed · not field validated

Singapore distributor inquiry

Regional distributor · commercial interior / building-material product · no live customer or external communication

AI does

Reconstructs context, compares evidence, finds gaps, drafts questions, updates state.

Human decides

Whether to continue, what risks to accept, and whether a quote or promise is justified.

System controls

Unknowns remain unknown; external sending and commercial commitment stay approval-gated.

01 / RFQ arrives

Start with the work as it arrives.

A real-looking request is rarely a complete decision. The first move is to preserve the original signal before anyone cleans it up.

From: Lina Tan <lina@meridian-build.sg>Subject: Inquiry — acoustic wall panels for Singapore projectReceived: Monday, 09:14 · forwarded by sales

Hi,

We are a regional distributor looking for acoustic wall panels for a commercial interior project in Singapore.

Could you advise availability and pricing for approximately 2,400 m²? Target delivery is late October. We would prefer a fire-rated finish and need samples soon.

Please let us know if you can support this.

Best,
Lina

Visible input

Product, estimated quantity, market, destination, and requested timing are present. A final specification, contracting entity, certification requirement, and payment terms are not.

02 / Context reconstruction

AI separates signal from missing context.

The system does not fill the gaps. It makes the gaps easier for a person to see.

Case state: reconstructedUnknowns preserved

Known

  • Acoustic wall panels
  • Approx. 2,400 m²
  • Singapore destination
  • Late-October target

Unknown

  • Final technical specification
  • Contracting entity / buyer role
  • Certification requirement
  • Payment terms

Conflict / verify

  • Delivery timing may conflict with an unresolved fire-rating and specification path
  • “Target” is not yet a binding date
AI does

Extracts, normalizes, and flags contradictions. It does not infer that the buyer is ready, qualified, or safe to quote.

03 / Qualification

Prepare the judgment. Do not replace it.

The inquiry is commercially relevant, but relevance is not readiness. The work now is to make the decision legible.

Commercial relevance

Regional distributor, plausible product category, meaningful estimated quantity.

Technical fit

Potentially suitable, but the final specification and fire-rating requirement remain open.

Resource exposure

Engineering review, sample preparation, production feasibility, and senior commercial attention.

Questions requiring confirmation

Buyer role, project status, certification, payment, and whether late October is required or preferred.

Human judgment required

Does this opportunity deserve more organizational attention before the evidence is complete?

Choose a bounded path

Continue discovery does not authorize a quotation or commercial commitment.

Human decides

Whether the opportunity deserves attention. Not whether the opportunity is won, quote-ready, or approved for external commitment.

04 / Evidence gap

AI prepares the minimum questions.

The machine absorbs coordination work. A human still reviews what the company is about to ask another party.

  • Confirm the final panel specification and fire-rating requirement.
  • Confirm the buyer or contracting entity for this project.
  • Confirm whether certification is mandatory and which standard applies.
  • Confirm payment terms and whether the target date is required or preferred.
  • Confirm project status: budgeted, specified, awarded, or exploratory.

Human review before evidence V1

AI has prepared the questions. This synthetic case now supplies confirmed answers for the first human decision. No external message has been sent.

System controls

Drafting is allowed. Sending is not. The external message remains a human-approved action.

Before

RFQ → read email → search messages → ask engineering → ask Founder → draft response → discover missing information later

With Human × AI

RFQ → reconstruct context → expose gaps → human judgment → evidence update → stale approval → human re-review → next action

Where attention is preserved

AI absorbs reading, extraction, comparison, context reconstruction, missing-information detection, drafting, and state updating. Human attention concentrates on commercial judgment, exceptions, risk, commitments, and authorization.

Prepared follow-up — awaiting human approval

“Thank you. We can continue the technical review. Before we prepare a conditional commercial response, could you confirm the impact of Rev B on the required fire-rating standard, payment terms, and delivery planning?”

What changed

Valid then. Not valid now. The evidence changed; the human decision had to be made again.
